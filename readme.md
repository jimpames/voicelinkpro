## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1 — Installation

unzip the file somewhere - desktop, downloads

Open `chrome://extensions`, enable **Developer Mode**, and click **Load Unpacked**. Select the `voicelinkpro` folder. Pin VoiceLink Pro to your toolbar so deployment is always one click away.

### Step 2 — Standby
Throughout the workday, leave VoiceLink Pro idle in your toolbar. The icon is unremarkable. Nobody will ask about it. If they do, say "it's a softphone for our European team." End of conversation.

### Step 3 — Activation
When pressure arrives — footsteps approaching, the phrase "got a sec?", a calendar invite titled simply "chat" — click the VoiceLink Pro icon. The softphone popup appears. Press **Call**.

Within 200 milliseconds, you are deep in Q3 financial review, currently on a call with Sarah, with AI-augmented frontend work visible one tab over. You are, by every visible metric, the employee of the month.

### Step 4 — Sustaining the Illusion
Cycle between Excel, Teams, Outlook, and VS Code at natural intervals (every 40–90 seconds is convincing). Squint at numbers. Nod occasionally. Murmur "mhm" if appropriate.

### Step 5 — De-escalation
When the threat has passed, click the call-in-progress widget in the top-right corner. The "call" ends. The tab closes. You return seamlessly to your actual work — Reddit, presumably.

## Advanced Tactics for Power Users

**The Cough-and-Mute.**
If approached during active deployment, cough lightly, mime hitting a mute button, and say *"sorry — give me one sec."* This buys an exit window of up to 90 seconds. Use it to walk somewhere, anywhere.

**The Pivot.**
If asked what you're working on, toggle to the Excel tab and announce *"I'm just finalizing the Q3 numbers."* This is a complete sentence. It generates no follow-up obligations.

**The Strategic Squint.**
While on the VS Code panel, lean slightly forward and squint at the screen. Approximately 73% of observers will assume you are debugging. The remaining 27% will assume you need glasses, which is also a win.

**The Confident Sigh.**
Exhale slowly while looking at the Outlook inbox. This communicates: *I am carrying a heavy load, and I am carrying it with dignity.* Do not break character.

**The Sarah Callback.**
Approximately 4 minutes into a deployment, glance at the call widget and mutter *"sorry Sarah, what was that?"* under your breath. This adds astonishing realism. Use sparingly.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Tabs don't switch | `panic.js` not loaded | Reload extension at `chrome://extensions` |
| "File moved or deleted" in app pane | iframe source missing | Confirm `excel.html`, `teams.html`, `outlook.html`, and `vscode.html` are in the extension folder |
| Call button does nothing | Stale `popup.js` referencing `panicBtn` | Verify the script targets `callBtn` |
| Manifest won't parse | You pasted JSON wrong, probably | Re-paste with the curly braces this time |
| Boss is now standing directly behind you | Beyond the design scope of this product | Refer to your company's HR documentation |
| Boss asks who Sarah is | Social engineering attempt | *"She's on the other team."* Always works. |
| Boss asks which team | Probing follow-up | Click End Call. Excuse yourself to a restroom. Do not return for 11 minutes. |
| You actually start to feel productive | Unintended side effect | Discontinue use immediately. Consult management. |
